🌸 Train Your Eye to See the Cars

Learn to read Japanese by finding the particles first. English relies on word order; Japanese relies on tiny glue-words called particles. These particles are like the cars of a train — they carry the meaning and connect to the engine (verb).

犬が 人を 噛んだ
人を 犬が 噛んだ
Both read "The dog bit the man." The particles anchor the roles.

How it works

  • You start at Level 1 with just 2 particles: が (subject) and を (object)
  • Get 5 perfect rounds to unlock the next level with more particles
  • Each level adds new particles and harder sentences
  • By Level 4, you'll be spotting all particles at character-level speed
